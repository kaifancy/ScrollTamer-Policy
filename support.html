Frequently Asked Questions

1. How does the daily tapering plan work?

ScrollTamer believes in sustainable habit changes rather than harsh cold-turkey blocks. On Days 1–2, a gentle buffer allows you to adapt. Starting on Day 3, your daily quota automatically tapers down by small, manageable increments toward your target goal.

2. How do the 5-minute extensions work?

When you reach today’s limit, a "Request 5 More Minutes" emergency button appears on the home screen. It unlocks your apps for a single 5-minute wind-down session. To prevent bingeing, only one extension is permitted per calendar day (unless Strict Mode is enabled, which disables extensions completely).

3. What should I do if an app is not being blocked?

Please check the following three steps:

  1. Open iOS Settings > Screen Time and ensure Screen Time is turned on.
  2. Open ScrollTamer and verify that Screen Time permission shows authorized and that the target application is selected.
  3. Briefly open ScrollTamer to let the background monitoring daemon refresh its schedule.

4. How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new phone?

Open ScrollTamer, go to Settings > Upgrade to Pro (or the Pro banner), and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom. Since purchases are tied directly to your Apple ID, your Pro access will restore immediately without charge.

5. How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

All subscription billing is handled directly by Apple. To manage or cancel your subscription:

  1. Open your iPhone Settings.
  2. Tap your Name / Apple ID at the top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Select ScrollTamer and tap Cancel Subscription.

6. Is any of my screen time data uploaded to a server?

No. Never. ScrollTamer is built with 100% on-device privacy. All time calculations and app limits run exclusively on your iPhone through Apple's sandboxed frameworks. We do not have servers, tracking databases, or third-party analytics.
